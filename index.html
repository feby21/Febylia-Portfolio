About me

My mission is to turn complex problems, from personal productivity to financial markets, into simple, data-driven products.

My degree combines finance with innovation, and the innovation side showed me how much I enjoy creating something new. Building the Love Bristol app prototype was the turning point: I found I loved solving problems through digital products.

Around the same time I was watching founders my age build start-ups with code and AI. I wanted to be able to do that too, so I started learning to code.

My other passion is markets. I enjoy working with data and reading about the stock market, so I'm developing my machine learning skills in Python and R to build data-driven frameworks for finance.
